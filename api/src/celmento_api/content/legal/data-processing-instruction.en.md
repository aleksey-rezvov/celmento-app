## 2. What client data you enter

- Name, photo, phone, email, Telegram, WhatsApp, other contacts.
- Date of birth, city, time zone, important dates.
- "Initial request", "Current goals".
- "About the client": family, profession, close people.
- "Hypotheses and observations": traits, test results, typology.
- Sessions, their outcomes, the specialist's comments.
- Payments, balance, rate, currency.
- Notes in which the client is tagged, including session records.
- Source: where the client came from.

A card created by a booking on your public page also holds the data the client entered themselves in the booking form: name, email, contact and comment.

## 3. How we process the data

- We store the data and show it to you in the Service.
- We show the client the data of the linked card: their sessions with you and the notes in which you tagged them.
- We send the client emails about a rescheduled or cancelled session — only to the email the client entered themselves when booking.

- We store the data on servers in Russia and do not pass it to the other region of the Service.

## 4. What we ensure

- The private card fields — "Initial request", "Current goals", "About the client", "Hypotheses and observations" — are never shown to the client, are not included in exports and are not accessible to the administrator.
- The administrator does not see notes, client cards, sessions or balances of specific people. All administrator actions are logged.
- The contacts you type into a card are for reference: we do not send emails or notifications to them, do not verify them and do not link accounts by them.

## 5. What you ensure

- You obtain your clients' consent to the processing of their data yourself and are responsible for the lawfulness of the data entered.
- The timesheet export contains clients' names and contacts. The Service warns about this before exporting. Passing such a file to third parties, for example to ICF, is your responsibility.
- "Send as text" to a messenger and "Add to calendar" move data outside the Service by your action. After that, you are responsible for the data.

## 6. Retention

- Clients' data, archived data included, is kept with no time limit until you delete your account.
- An individual client card cannot be deleted yet — there is only the archive; we plan to add deletion. [Legal: how to fulfil a client's request to delete their data while there is no deletion of an individual card (question 21)]
