## 2. Plans

- Free plan: all features of the Service, up to 3 active clients. Archived clients do not count towards the limit.
- Monthly or yearly subscription: all features of the Service with no limit on active clients.
- Future features — session transcription, recording storage, an AI helper — are shown on the plans screen marked "Coming soon". [Legal: wording of the "Coming soon" mark without a commitment to include these features in the current subscription (question 6)]
- Changing the plan does not delete data: clients, sessions, hours and amounts are kept.

- A year costs the same as 8 months.
- Discounts do not add up: the lowest price the account is entitled to applies.
- Prices are reviewed once a quarter. A review does not change the price of a period already paid.
- A subscription taken out in one region does not extend to the same person's account in the other region. Partner codes work in the region where they were issued.

### 3.1. Promo price

- The promo price applies to payments made during a promotion, for the paid period, including renewal of an existing subscription.
- The next renewal is at the base price, unless a new promotion is running at that moment.

### 3.2. Partner price

- The partner price is given with a partner code. A code is activated once per account, has an activation period and a usage limit; the partner may restrict the code to a list of emails.
- The price is kept as long as the subscription is renewed without a break, including when switching between monthly and yearly.
- When the account moves to the free plan, the partner price is lost, and the code cannot be entered again.
- Disabling a code does not cancel prices already locked in.

## 4. Subscription granted by the administrator

- The administrator may grant a subscription until a set date. After that date, the usual rules apply.

## 5. End of a subscription

- If more than 3 clients are active when the subscription ends, you choose in advance the three who stay active.
- If no choice is made, the other clients move to the archive by the default rule. Their planned sessions are deleted, and the clients receive cancellation notifications. Past hours and amounts are kept.
