## 2. Whose data we process

- People who work with clients in the Service.
- People with an account whom specialists work with.
- Guests of the public booking page and people who open a meeting page.
- Specialists' clients without an account — their data was entered by the specialist.
- Partners' contact persons.
- Partners' graduates and employees, if the partner has provided a list of emails. [Legal: the basis for a partner providing a list of graduates' and employees' emails (question 15)]

### Client data entered by the specialist

- Name, photo, phone, email, Telegram, WhatsApp, other contacts.
- Date of birth, city, time zone, important dates.
- "Initial request", "Current goals".
- "About the client": family, profession, close people.
- "Hypotheses and observations": traits, test results, typology.
- Sessions, their outcomes, the specialist's comments.
- Payments, balance, rate, currency.
- Notes in which the client is tagged, including session records.
- Source: where the client came from.
- The email the client entered themselves when booking on the public page — kept apart from the contacts the specialist typed in.

The specialist is the operator of their clients' data. We process it under the data processing instruction for clients' data, which the specialist accepts when turning on "Start working with clients", and only to run the Service. The specialist obtains the clients' consent to processing themselves and is responsible for the lawfulness of the data entered. The private card fields are never shown to the client, are not included in exports and are not accessible to the administrator.

The contacts a specialist types into a card are for reference: we do not send emails to them, do not verify them and do not link accounts by them. A card is linked to the client's account automatically — by the email the client entered themselves when booking — or by the login the client told the specialist themselves. The client receives a notification about the connection and can unlink.

## 5. Who we share data with

- The specialist — the data of the guest or client who booked with them.
- The specialist who invited a person to a meeting — the name and login of the new account, if the "Notify" checkbox for that specialist stayed ticked at registration from the meeting page.
- The client — the data from the linked card to the extent described in the Terms of Use: sessions with that specialist and notes in which they are tagged.
- Contractors: hosting, email delivery service, and in the future a payment provider. [Fill in after the team's choice: contractors — hosting, email delivery service, payment provider]
- The administrator's AI assistant — only public profiles, feedback and anonymised statistics. [Fill in: the administrator's AI provider] [Legal: cross-border transfer of data to the AI provider and other contractors (question 17)]
- The partner — only the anonymised number of activations of their code. [Legal: confirm that the partner receives only the anonymised number of activations]
- No data is passed to Telegram, WhatsApp, Google Meet or other apps: the Service only opens those apps on the device.
- By your action, data may leave the Service: "Send as text" to a messenger, "Add to calendar", the timesheet export. ## 6. Where data is stored

- The Service runs in two independent regions: "Russia" (celmento.ru) and "Rest of the world" (celmento.com). The regions share no account database and no email directory, and exchange no data.
- The user chooses the region when finishing registration; a person who came by a specialist's link or a meeting link gets the specialist's region. The connection country and IP address play no part in this choice. The region cannot be changed later.

## 9. How privacy works

- Any note and any field is visible only to its author. Another person gets access to a note only through an explicit tag in it; removing the tag closes access immediately.
- The private client card fields — "Initial request", "Current goals", "About the client", "Hypotheses and observations" — are never visible to the client under any circumstances.
- A client's specialists do not see each other or each other's data.
- The administrator does not see notes, client cards, sessions or balances of specific people. They can access public profiles, feedback, anonymised statistics and technical account details. All administrator actions are logged.
- The email of a feedback author is shown to the administrator only on a separate request.
- We plan: an app lock with a PIN or biometrics; a "Hide details in notifications" setting; confirmation of the card link by the client; a hint under the private card fields, "Record only what the client has consented to", and a hint not to write clients' names in "What can we improve?"; deletion of an individual client card.

## 10. Things worth stating separately

- There are no automated decisions that affect you. The exception is the automatic archiving of clients when a subscription ends, by a rule the specialist can change in advance.
- Geolocation is not used. The device's time zone is used to show the time.
- AI features for users, such as session transcription, will come later. We will update the policy before launching them. For now, only the administrator has an AI assistant, see section 5.
