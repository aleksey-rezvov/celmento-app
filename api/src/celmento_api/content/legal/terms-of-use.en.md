## 1. What this document is about

These Terms describe how you use celmēnto (the "Service"): a web app for the phone screen, a planner for people-helping professionals, and the public web pages — for booking a session with a specialist and for a specific meeting — which open in a browser without installing anything and without signing in.

The Service runs in two independent regions, each with its own set of documents.

Terms:

- Account — a user account in the Service, identified by an email within a region.
- Specialist — the person who works with someone in a specific connection. An account can work with clients once "Start working with clients" is turned on in it.
- Client — the person a specialist works with in a specific connection.
- Connection — the link between a client card kept by a specialist and that client's account.
- Guest — a person without an account who books a session with a specialist on the public page.

One account can work with its own clients and attend a specialist itself: these are not different roles but different connections.

The Terms apply to everyone who uses the Service. You accept them when you tick "I accept the Terms of Use" at registration or when booking a session with a specialist as a guest. Sections 2–11 apply to everyone, section 12 to those who work with clients, section 13 to those a specialist works with, section 14 to guests.

Other documents apply alongside these Terms:

The website celmento.ru, the video-call service meet.celmento.ru and the Telegram channel t.me/celmento are not built into the app and have their own documents.

## 2. Account and sign-in

- Your account is your email in the chosen region. There is no password: when you sign in, we email you a one-time 6-digit code. The code is valid for 10 minutes, and you have 5 attempts to enter it.
- You are responsible for access to your email: whoever can read your email can sign in to your account.
- You choose the region yourself when finishing registration. If you came by a specialist's link or a meeting link, the region is the specialist's region. The region cannot be changed later.
- One email can hold one account in each region. Such accounts are independent: data and records are not merged between them.
- Until you tap "Finish registration", we create no records about you: an unfinished registration is kept only on your device for 24 hours.
- You stay signed in until you sign out yourself. There is no app lock with a PIN yet — we plan to add a PIN or biometric lock. So you are responsible for protecting your device.
- You may attend specialists and book with them on your own from the age of 14, and younger with the consent of a legal representative; the Service does not check age. Working with clients is allowed only from the age of 18: this is confirmed when turning on "Start working with clients". [Legal: confirm the age condition (question 2)]

## 3. What the Service does and does not do

The Service is a tool for organising a practice and keeping records: sessions, clients, notes, hours and payments. To a person a specialist works with, the Service shows the sessions with that specialist and the notes shared with them.

What the Service does not do:

- it does not provide psychological, medical or consulting services and is not responsible for the quality of the specialists' services;
- it is not a party to the relationship between a specialist and a client;
- it does not verify specialists' diplomas, certificates or qualifications — the "Education and certificates" section is filled in by the specialist;
- it does not accept or transfer money for sessions: balances and payments in the Service are reference records kept by the specialist;
- it does not host calls: they take place in third-party apps — phone, Telegram, WhatsApp, Google Meet, Zoom. The Service only opens those apps and is not responsible for how they work or for their confidentiality;
- it is not intended for emergency help. [Legal: the "not for emergency help" wording and links to crisis lines in "Help" (question 23)]

What else is important to know:

- The record of hours and payments is for reference. It does not replace accounting or tax records.
- The timesheet export, including the format compatible with the ICF log, is built from your data. Check it before submitting it to certifying organisations.
- The Service is not affiliated with professional associations, including ICF, unless there is a separate agreement with them. [Legal: use of the ICF name and the names of other associations and schools (question 24)]
- The Service works only with an internet connection.

## 4. Your content

- Notes, client cards, sessions and other data are created by you, and the rights to them remain yours. We only need permission (a licence) to store and display this content to the extent necessary for the Service to work.
- The pre-created notes — "Ethics codes", "Libraries", "Communities and associations", "Books and films", "Affirmations", the session record template — appear when you turn on "Start working with clients" and are the Service's content. You can edit and delete them. External links in them lead to third-party resources.
- Tagging a person in a note opens it to that person for reading. Removing the tag closes access immediately. You are responsible for what you share.
- We may use the "What can we improve?" feedback to improve the product. Please do not write clients' names in it.
- "Send as text" to a messenger, "Add to calendar" on your phone and the timesheet export move data outside the Service by your action. After that, you are responsible for the data.

## 5. Public visibility

- Only an account with "Start working with clients" turned on has a public booking page. It is available to anyone who has the link. It shows the photo, name, specialisation, "About me", price (if the specialist has enabled it), format, cancellation policy and free time.
- A meeting page is available to anyone who has the link the specialist shared. It shows the specialist's name, photo and specialisation, the date, time, duration and format of the meeting, and the call link. The client's name, the price and comments are not shown on it.
- Listing in the directory is only by the specialist's choice: the "Show me in the directory" switch is off by default.
- Every account has a login, unique within its region. The login follows rules like a Telegram username; reserved words are not allowed. When the login changes, the old link stops working.

## 6. Free plan, limit and archive

- Free: up to 3 active clients. Archived clients do not count towards the limit.
- A monthly or yearly subscription removes the limit; its terms are in the specialist offer. Subscription payment does not work yet: the "I want a subscription" button saves a request, and no contract is formed.
- Archive: past hours, amounts and notes are kept, and an archived card opens read-only. Planned sessions are deleted on archiving, and clients receive cancellation notifications. Before archiving manually, the specialist marks the outcomes of past sessions.
- When the limit is reached, a new person cannot book via the public link. The specialist receives a notification with that person's name.

## 7. Notifications

- We send only service emails: the sign-in code, the booking confirmation to a guest, a session rescheduled or cancelled.
- Emails about a rescheduled or cancelled session go only to the email the person entered themselves when booking with the specialist. We do not write to contacts the specialist typed into the card.
- Other notifications are shown in the app.
- There are no marketing mailings in the Service. If they appear, we will ask for a separate consent.

## 8. Signing out and deleting the account

- "Sign out" ends the session on the device. The data is kept.
- "Delete account" is irreversible and is confirmed with a code. The account, client cards, sessions, notes and records are deleted. People you shared notes with lose access to them.
- If a person a specialist works with deletes their account, their card stays with the specialist: it is the specialist's working record, and only the connection disappears. [Legal: what happens to the card at the specialist after the client deletes their account (question 3)]

## 9. Administration

- The administrator manages the Service through an AI assistant, separately in each region. They can access only public profiles, feedback, anonymised statistics and technical account details: registration date, whether the account works with clients, plan, number of active clients.
- Notes, client cards, sessions and balances of specific people are not accessible to the administrator.
- The administrator can hide a specialist's profile from the directory, stating the reason. Booking via the link keeps working.
- All administrator actions are logged.

## 12. If you work with clients

- Working with clients is turned on with "Start working with clients" at registration or later in "Profile". To do so, you confirm "I am 18 or older" and tick "I accept the specialist terms and the data processing instruction" — as separate checkboxes. The date, time and document versions are stored in the account.
- It cannot be turned off: unused sections simply stay empty.
- You are the operator of your clients' data. We process it on your instruction and only to run the Service. You obtain the clients' consent to processing yourself and are responsible for the lawfulness of the data entered.
- The contacts you type into a client card are for your reference. We do not send emails or notifications to them, do not verify them and do not link accounts by them.
- A card is linked to the client's account in two ways: automatically — by the email the client entered themselves when booking on your public page; manually — by the login the client told you themselves. Either side can unlink; the card, history and records stay with you.

## 13. If a specialist works with you

- With each specialist you see only the data of the linked card: your sessions with them and the notes in which they tagged you. The private card fields — "Initial request", "Current goals", "About the client", "Hypotheses and observations" — and the specialist's comments on sessions are not shown to you.
- A session can be cancelled or rescheduled only through the specialist. The cancellation policy is set by the specialist, who also decides whether to charge for a late cancellation.
- Your reply to a note is visible to its author. Ticks on checklist items in a note are visible to its author.
- Your specialists do not see each other or each other's data.
- A card at a specialist is linked to your account automatically if you entered this email yourself when booking with them and registered with it in their region, or by the login you told them yourself. You receive a notification about the connection and can unlink at any time. ## 14. If you are a guest of the public page

- You book without an account. The form asks for: name (a nickname is fine), email, a contact for reaching you (optional), session format and a comment.
- Your data is passed to the specialist you are booking with: a client card and a session are created for them.
- If the booking did not go through because of the specialist's limit or archive, the specialist still receives a notification with your name.
- You receive a booking confirmation with a link to the app at the email you entered, and an email if the specialist reschedules or cancels the session. To reschedule or cancel a session, contact the specialist.
- Times on the page are shown in your device's time zone. Geolocation is not used.
