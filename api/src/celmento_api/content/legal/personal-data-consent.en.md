## 2. User consent: registration in the app

By ticking the checkbox at registration, you agree that we process your data as described below.

### 2.1. What data

- Account: email, first name, last name, language, time zone, region, creation date, whether working with clients is turned on.
- Sign-in: one-time codes, when they were sent, number of attempts.
- Consents and confirmations: document, version, date and time of acceptance; the "I am 18 or older" confirmation, if you turned on "Start working with clients".
- Profile: photo, first name, last name, phone, Telegram, WhatsApp, login.
- If you work with clients, also: specialisation, "About me", education and certificates, contacts, social networks, working hours, session format, office address, meeting link, rate, currency, cancellation policy.
- Working data: sessions and their outcomes, notes, tags, checklist items, balance operations, timesheets, hour goals. This includes sessions of the "Personal therapy" and "Supervision" types — only you can see them.
- Commercial data: plan, subscription, subscription requests, partner code, locked-in price.
- "What can we improve?" feedback: text, whether the author works with clients, screen, date.
- IP address and connection country — for statistics and search optimisation. They are not used in registration, sign-in or the choice of region.

The data of your clients that you enter into cards and notes when you work with clients is not the subject of this consent. Its processing is described in the data processing instruction for clients' data, which you accept when turning on "Start working with clients". [Legal: health information that may end up in notes and cards is a special category of personal data; requirements for consent and protection (question 9)]

### 2.2. What for

Providing the Service; booking sessions with a specialist; service notifications; recording hours and payments; subscription and partner programme; support; anonymised statistics and product improvement; directory moderation.

### 2.3. Who we share data with

- The specialist you have booked with — the data of your booking.
- The specialist who invited you to a meeting — your name and login, if you left the "Notify" checkbox for that specialist ticked when registering from the meeting page.
- A person you work with in a connection — the data from the linked card, to the extent described in the Terms of Use.
- Contractors: hosting, email delivery service, and in the future a payment provider. [Fill in after the team's choice: contractors — hosting, email delivery service, payment provider]
- The administrator's AI assistant — only public profiles, feedback and anonymised statistics. [Fill in: the administrator's AI provider] [Legal: cross-border transfer of data to the AI provider and other contractors (question 17)]
- The partner whose code you activated — only the anonymised number of code activations. [Legal: confirm that the partner receives only the anonymised number of activations]
- No data is passed to Telegram, WhatsApp, Google Meet or other apps: the Service only opens them on your device.

### 2.4. For how long

- Account and working data — until you delete your account. The client archive — indefinitely, as long as the specialist's account exists.
- Sign-in codes are valid for 10 minutes. [Legal: retention period for sign-in code records (question 13)]
- Records of accepted consents and their versions — [Legal: retention period for consent records and their versions (question 13)].

- View and correct your data — in "Profile" and cards.
- Delete your account — "Delete account" in "Profile", confirmed with a code. This is irreversible: the account, client cards, sessions, notes and records are deleted.
- Withdraw your consent and contact us with any question about your data — section 2.6.

## 3. Guest consent: booking on the public page

By ticking the checkbox in the booking form, you agree that we process your data as described below.

### 3.1. What data

- Form data: name (a nickname is fine), email, a contact for reaching you (if you provided one), session format, comment.
- Your device's time zone — to show the time. Geolocation is not used.
- Consents: document, version, date and time of acceptance — they are stored together with the booking.

### 3.2. What for

- To book you with the specialist and pass your data to them: a client card and a session are created for the specialist.
- To notify the specialist of your request even if the booking did not go through because of their limit or archive — the notification contains your name.
- To email you at the address you entered about the booking: a confirmation with a link to the app, emails about rescheduling or cancellation.
- [Legal: to whom the guest gives consent — the Service, the specialist or both (question 5)]

### 3.3. Who we share data with

- The specialist you are booking with.
- Contractors — the same as in section 2.3: hosting and the email delivery service.

### 3.4. For how long

- Your data is kept in the client card at the specialist: as long as their account exists, and indefinitely in the archive.
- If you later register with the same email in the specialist's region, the card is linked to your account, and section 2 applies from then on.

### 3.5. Your rights and withdrawing consent

- You can correct your data through the specialist, who edits the card, or by writing to us: [Fill in: contact for requests in the region].
- To withdraw consent, write to us at the same contact. A specialist cannot yet delete an individual client card — there is only the archive; we plan to add deletion. [Legal: how to fulfil a request to delete data at the specialist while there is no deletion of an individual card (question 21)]
